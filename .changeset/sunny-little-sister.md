New `character` option and her little sister `sunny` (`import { sunny } from '@d4ndel1on/pagegeist/sunny'`): same black hair, the goth's hand-me-downs bleached ghostly white, with light speech bubbles, a ghostly glow on dark pages and a blue-grey outline on light ones and her own friendly, attention-seeking phrases (`SUNNY_PHRASES`). `goth` stays the default and is exported from the main entry. `outlineColor` now defaults to the character's color, and the pause button background now uses the same color as the speech bubble (slightly more opaque).

The sisters tease each other: seven new built-in phrases for `goth` (in `DEFAULT_PHRASES`) and for `sunny`. The docs get a "Pick a sister" guide for choosing between them. `sunny` is also more eager: she notices the cursor from further away, walks over to it at a brisker pace and takes shorter breaks. The artwork license (CC0) now names both sisters.
